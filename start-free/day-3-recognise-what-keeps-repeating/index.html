Recognise What Keeps Repeating

Today's teaching

Repeated outcomes often have a repeated mental pattern behind them: an assumption, interpretation or familiar response. Recognising the pattern gives you useful information; it does not require you to blame yourself.

What to observe today

When a familiar situation appears today, look for the first thought that follows it and the behaviour that usually comes next.

Your reflection

Which repeated thought seems to lead towards a response you would like to change?

One practical action

Write one pattern as a simple sequence: trigger, thought, response. Add one alternative response you could try next time.
