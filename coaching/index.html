Frequently Asked Questions

How is the coaching delivered?

The journey combines progressive weekly study, practical exercises, reflection and guidance from Tariq. Your exact session arrangements are confirmed when you enrol.

How much time should I allow?

Set aside consistent time each week for the lesson, its exercise and your own reflection. Tariq can help you shape a realistic rhythm.

What if I miss a week?

Return to the current lesson without judging yourself. The emphasis is on steady application, not rushing to catch up.

Can I work at my own pace?

The sequence is designed to unfold progressively. Discuss timing with Tariq before changing the weekly rhythm so you preserve that progression.

Is this programme independent?

Yes. This is an independent coaching experience inspired by the Master Key System. It is not endorsed by or affiliated with Charles F. Haanel.

How do enrolment and payment work?

Contact Tariq to discuss the right option. Availability, confirmation and payment are then arranged through the existing approved process.
