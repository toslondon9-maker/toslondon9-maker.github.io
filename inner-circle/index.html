More than a game night

Good company. Bigger conversations. New perspectives.

The Inner Circle is a small, carefully curated Barcelona community for people interested in investing, entrepreneurship, financial intelligence and personal development. It is a place to exchange ideas, challenge assumptions, share experiences and enjoy the company of people who are genuinely interested in learning from one another.

A signature shared experience

Cashflow 101 & Cashflow 202

Created by Robert Kiyosaki, these interactive board games turn financial concepts into a lively, hands-on experience. Play together, make decisions, discuss the outcomes and explore the thinking behind the choices.

They give the group a common starting point for thoughtful conversations about cash flow, assets, liabilities, risk and the assumptions we bring to financial decisions.

Assets put money in your pocket.Liabilities take money out.

The games are educational conversation tools, not financial or investment advice. Members are encouraged to think independently and make their own decisions.
