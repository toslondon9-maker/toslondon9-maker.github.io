The Inner Circle is a small, curated Barcelona community for people who enjoy exchanging ideas, exploring entrepreneurship and developing a more considered approach to financial decisions. It is a place to connect, learn through experience and enjoy time together.

Think differently about money

Cashflow 101 & Cashflow 202

Created by Robert Kiyosaki, these interactive board games turn financial concepts into a shared, hands-on experience. Play, make decisions, discuss the outcomes and explore how cash flow works.

One central distinction runs through the game:

Assets put money in your pocket.Liabilities take money out.

The games are educational tools for discussion, not financial or investment advice.
