A signature shared experience

The CASHFLOW Board Game

Created by Robert and Kim Kiyosaki, the current CASHFLOW Board Game turns financial concepts into a lively, hands-on experience. Play together, make decisions, discuss the outcomes and explore the thinking behind the choices.

The game gives the group a common starting point for thoughtful conversations about cash flow, assets, liabilities, risk and the assumptions we bring to financial decisions. Each player receives a profession and completes a personal financial statement, then makes choices as opportunities and unexpected expenses arise.
