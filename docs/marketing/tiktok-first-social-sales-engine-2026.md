# UYP TikTok-first social sales engine — launch plan
Prepared: 7 November 2026 | Launch horizon: through 31 December 2026

## Objective
TikTok is the primary discovery and lead-generation engine. The UYP website and email sequence own conversion. Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, LinkedIn and X extend reach and trust without creating six separate full-time content operations.

Offer ladder: free 7-Day Experience → Foundation paid step where appropriate → premium 24-week / small-group coaching for qualified prospects. The AI Master Key System Study Companion supports engagement and demonstrates the learning ecosystem; it does not replace coaching.

Positioning: Practical focus and mindset coaching inspired by Charles F. Haanel’s Master Key System — guided study, daily exercises and accountability to help people think more clearly and act more deliberately.

## Audience and content pillars
Primary audience: English-speaking adults in the UK and internationally who want better focus, concentration, self-discipline, clarity and purposeful action.
1. Focus and attention: distraction, single-tasking, concentration.
2. Overthinking and self-direction: pause, observe, decide, act.
3. Discipline and consistency: small commitments and routines.
4. Master Key System education: Haanel's ideas, lessons and exercise demonstrations.
5. Practical stories: Tariq's experience and genuine participant stories with explicit permission.
6. Coaching and accountability: inclusions, fit, objections and next steps.
7. Study Companion demos: genuine screen recordings and real features only.

Tone: confident, direct, thoughtful, warm and grounded. Avoid instant transformation, guaranteed wealth/health, miracle claims or therapy/treatment claims. Separate philosophical claims in Haanel's text from evidence-based behavioural claims.

## TikTok creative framework
Default video: 25–40 seconds. First 0–2 seconds: hook; 2–8 seconds: recognisable situation; 8–28 seconds: one useful idea or exercise; 28–35 seconds: one next step. One video, one job, one CTA.
Film 9:16 vertical, ideally 1080×1920. Use readable captions, natural clear audio, safe zones and platform-cleared music. No long logo intro. Test alternative hooks for the same lesson.
Measure watch time, completion, saves, shares, profile visits, link clicks and confirmed registrations — not views alone.

## Five ready-to-record TikTok scripts

### 1. Divided attention
On-screen hook: You might not have a motivation problem. You might have an attention problem.
Script: Think about the last hour. How many times did you switch tasks, check your phone or start something new before finishing the first thing? Every switch pulls your attention away from what matters. Try this today: choose one important task, put your phone out of reach and give it ten focused minutes. When your mind wanders, gently bring it back. That is a practice. If you want a structured introduction to focus and mental discipline, start my free 7-Day Experience.
Caption: Small practice. One clear task. Ten minutes. Start with the free 7-Day Experience.
CTA: The free 7-Day Experience is linked in my bio.

### 2. 60-second concentration exercise
On-screen hook: Can you give one idea your full attention for 60 seconds?
Script: Choose a simple object in front of you. Look at it carefully. Notice its shape, colour and details. When your mind jumps to another thought, notice that and return to the object. Do not criticise yourself. The return is the exercise. Try it for one minute today, then repeat tomorrow. Consistency matters more than perfection. I teach practical exercises like this through my free seven-day introduction.
Caption: Concentration is practised, not wished into existence.
CTA: Try the free 7-Day Experience — link in bio.

### 3. What the Master Key System is
On-screen hook: The Master Key System is not a magic button.
Script: Charles F. Haanel's Master Key System is a 24-part study of thought, attention, visualisation and deliberate practice. Reading the words is only the beginning. The value comes from studying an idea, completing the exercise and considering how it applies to your choices. My role as a coach is to help make that process structured and practical. Start with the free seven-day introduction if you want to experience the approach.
Caption: Study. Practise. Reflect. Apply. One week at a time.
CTA: Free 7-Day Experience in my bio.

### 4. Reacting versus choosing
On-screen hook: Before you answer that message, take one breath.
Script: A message arrives. You feel irritated. Your fingers are ready to reply. Pause. Take one deliberate breath. Ask yourself: what response would serve the outcome I actually want? You may still need to be firm. But you have created a moment to choose instead of reacting automatically. Practise that pause today. It is a small way to become more deliberate.
Caption: The pause gives you a chance to choose your response.
CTA: Follow for practical focus and mindset exercises.

### 5. Motivation versus practice
On-screen hook: Motivation is useful. A repeatable practice is more dependable.
Script: Most people know the feeling of starting something with enthusiasm and then losing momentum. Instead of setting a huge target, choose a small action you can repeat: ten minutes of focused study, a short reflection or one exercise. Make it easy to begin and track whether you showed up. That is how you build consistency through action, not slogans. If you want a guided starting point, the free 7-Day Experience is available through my bio.
Caption: Make the next step small enough to repeat.
CTA: Start the free 7-Day Experience.

## Tracking and attribution
Use consistent campaign UTMs: utm_source=tiktok|instagram|youtube|facebook|linkedin|x; utm_medium=organic_social; utm_campaign=uyp_7day_launch. Each platform gets its own source value.
Track profile views, bio-link clicks, landing sessions, registration starts, confirmed registrations, Day 7 completions, calls booked/attended, qualified enquiries and verified paid sales.
Rates: profile-to-link = link clicks/profile views; landing conversion = confirmed registrations/unique landing sessions; lead-to-call = attended calls/confirmed registrations; call-to-client = verified new clients/attended calls; revenue per lead = verified attributable revenue/confirmed leads.
Never count views, likes, checkout clicks or email opens as sales. Do not send personal form details into analytics. Reconcile paid enrolments against payment-provider records.

1. Review comments, search suggestions and analytics.
2. Select five audience questions/problems.
3. Write five hooks and scripts.
4. Batch-record in one session.
5. Edit clean vertical masters with captions.
6. Publish natively to TikTok and adapt to other channels.
7. Reply to meaningful comments; turn useful questions into video replies.
8. Review the funnel from view to confirmed registration to paid sale.

Monthly: identify top three topics by confirmed registrations and qualified conversations, not views; test new hooks against proven topics; refresh pinned videos; review free-experience and email drop-off. Only test paid TikTok amplification after organic creative and registration tracking show credible conversion. Set a capped budget and maximum cost per confirmed lead before spending.

## Assumptions and limits
This plan does not assume accounts have been created, handles reserved, profiles completed, link-in-bio access enabled, ad accounts approved or platform integrations connected. Those require confirmation within each platform. It does not promise reach, viral growth, registrations or sales; it establishes a testable system to improve them.
