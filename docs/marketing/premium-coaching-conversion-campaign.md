# UYP Premium Coaching Conversion Campaign

## Purpose

Create one coherent path from discovery to a qualified conversation about the Complete 24-Week Journey:

**Helpful content → Free 7-Day Experience → useful follow-up → coaching conversation → Complete 24-Week Journey (£997).**

The campaign is designed for English-speaking adults in the UK and internationally who want greater focus, concentration, mental discipline and purposeful achievement. Explain the Master Key System accurately and avoid promising guaranteed outcomes.

## Offer positioning

### Core promise

A structured 24-week coaching and study journey inspired by Charles F. Haanel's *The Master Key System*, helping participants study the principles, practise the exercises consistently, reflect on their experience and apply what they learn to meaningful goals.

### Who it is for

- People who want to develop more consistent focus and concentration.
- People who have goals but struggle to translate intention into disciplined action.
- People who value structured study, practical exercises and personal guidance.
- People willing to practise regularly and take responsibility for their progress.

### Who it is not for

- Anyone expecting instant results or guaranteed life, health or financial outcomes.
- Anyone unwilling to engage with the weekly study and exercises.
- Anyone seeking clinical or therapeutic treatment.

### What is included

Present only confirmed programme features in public copy:
- A structured 24-week study journey through the Master Key System.
- Weekly practical exercises, reflection and application.
- Coaching guidance and opportunities to discuss progress with Tariq.
- Access to the UYP AI Master Key System Study Companion as a supplementary learning aid.
- A clear progression through the programme's existing four study stages.

Before publishing any specific call frequency, WhatsApp access, group size, session duration or bonus, confirm that the delivery commitment is approved and operational. Do not imply that AI replaces coaching or that the AI companion is endorsed by Haanel.

### Price and call to action

- Complete 24-Week Journey: **£997**.
- Primary CTA: **Apply to discuss the 24-Week Journey**.
- Secondary CTA: **Start the free 7-Day Experience**.
- Do not add urgency, scarcity, discounts or income/achievement guarantees without substantiation.

## Coaching page content architecture

1. **Hero:** “Build the focus to turn intention into purposeful action.” Explain that this is a guided 24-week Master Key System study and coaching journey. Primary CTA: Apply to discuss the journey. Secondary CTA: Start free.
2. **The challenge:** Describe familiar experiences—scattered attention, inconsistent routines, too many intentions and difficulty following through—without diagnosing visitors.
3. **The approach:** Explain the study-practise-reflect-apply rhythm and the role of repetition and personal responsibility.
4. **The 24-week pathway:** Use the existing programme stages and approved week ranges. For each stage, explain its purpose, practice and practical application using the current curriculum; do not invent lesson content.
5. **What support looks like:** Clearly distinguish self-study resources, AI study support and direct coaching. State only confirmed delivery details.
6. **The AI Study Companion:** Show how it can help learners ask questions, explore ideas, reflect and revisit exercises between study sessions. Include an honest note that it is an independent educational aid, not an authority or substitute for the original text or coaching.
7. **Who it suits / may not suit:** Use the fit criteria above.
8. **About Tariq:** Use verified biography and motivation only. Avoid invented credentials or client results.
9. **Evidence and trust:** Add testimonials only with permission and exact approved wording. Until then, use a transparent explanation of the programme, sample exercise and real product screenshots—not fabricated testimonials or outcome claims.
10. **Investment and next step:** State £997 and explain the application/conversation step. Link to the existing secure and tested enquiry/checkout route; do not create a new payment route without testing.
11. **FAQ:** Cover time commitment, online delivery, what the AI companion does, what it does not do, suitability, price, support and refunds using approved operational and policy details.
