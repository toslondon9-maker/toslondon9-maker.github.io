# UYP Paid-Customer Email Sequences

Drafts for three paid customer journeys. These are copy drafts only; they are not active automations.

## Automation rules

- Foundation purchase: start the Foundation sequence and stop free-lead sales emails.
- Complete 24-Week Journey purchase: start the 24-week sequence and stop free-lead sales emails.
- Small-group purchase: start the group sequence and stop free-lead sales emails.
- Use the actual purchase date and programme start date to schedule milestones.
- Prevent duplicate sends if a purchase webhook or trigger is repeated.
- Respect marketing consent and unsubscribe preferences; keep essential transactional messages separate.
- Use placeholders for dates, Zoom links and delivery details until confirmed.
- If a customer purchases another programme, enrol them in the additional sequence without duplicating unrelated emails.

Keep going.
