The 24 Master Key System Exercises

A straightforward week-by-week checklist of the exercises in the 24-week Master Key System study sequence. Read the corresponding lesson, practise its exercise regularly during the week, and note what you observe.

Open the 24-week Study Room
  1. Week 1 of 24

    One Consciousness - One Power

    Sit upright and comfortably in a quiet room for 15–30 minutes. Allow thoughts to roam, but keep the body perfectly still. Practise daily until physical stillness becomes natural.

  2. Week 2 of 24

    One Method of Finding the Truth

    Use the same place and posture as Week 1. Be perfectly still and gently inhibit thought. Each time care, worry or fear enters, release it and return to mental quiet.

  3. Week 3 of 24

    Thoughts become Things

    Be perfectly still and inhibit thought as far as possible. Then relax completely: let every muscle and nerve return to its natural condition until you feel quiet, restful and at peace with yourself and the world.

  4. Week 4 of 24

    The true “Self”

    Relax physically, then mentally let go of hatred, anger, worry, jealousy, envy, sorrow, disappointment and every adverse condition. Release them through deliberate intention and persistence.

  5. Week 5 of 24

    The Brain of Man

    Select a place with pleasant associations. Re-create it mentally in complete detail—the buildings, grounds, trees, people, light, colour and atmosphere. Return to the same image every day.

  6. Week 6 of 24

    From Awareness to Success

    Choose a small photograph. Study every detail for ten minutes, then close your eyes and reproduce it mentally. Repeat until the inner image is clear and accurate.

  7. Week 7 of 24

    The Power of your Imagination

    Visualise a friend exactly as last seen: the room, furniture, conversation, face and expression. Mentally speak with them and watch their expression change naturally.

  8. Week 8 of 24

    The Value of Truthful Thinking

    Choose a familiar manufactured object and trace it mentally back to its origin. Follow the design, raw materials, people, tools, transport and sequence of work that brought it into form.

  9. Week 9 of 24

    Action as the Pinnacle of Thought

    Visualise a favourite flower from seed to full bloom. See the seed planted, watered and warmed; watch the shoot, stem, leaves and flower unfold in a continuous sequence.

  10. Week 10 of 24

    Life in Harmony with Natural Law

    On a blank wall, mentally draw a six-inch black horizontal line. Add two vertical lines and a top line to form a square; then imagine a circle inside it and a central point. Build and remove each element clearly.

  11. Week 11 of 24

    Inductive Reasoning

    Concentrate on the principle: whatever worthy thing you desire, believe inwardly that it is possible and received in principle. Then identify the thought and action that would agree with that belief.

  12. Week 12 of 24

    Understanding the Spiritual Nature of Thought

    Relax completely in your usual place. Contemplate your unity with the source of intelligence and your ability to think, choose and act. Hold the idea calmly, without physical or mental pressure.

  13. Week 13 of 24

    The Law of Cause and Effect

    Recognise that the directing ‘I’ is a part of the greater whole and therefore shares its qualities in degree. Sit with this idea until it becomes a felt principle, then write the action it inspires.

  14. Week 14 of 24

    The Discipline of Thinking

    Concentrate deeply on harmony until it is the dominant awareness. Do not merely repeat the word—feel order in the body, thought, relationships and intended actions.

  15. Week 15 of 24

    Conscious Cooperation with the Omnipotent

    Concentrate on insight. Choose one present problem and distinguish knowledge from habit. Formulate a definite programme for applying one known principle to that problem this week.

  16. Week 16 of 24

    Creating Scientifically True Ideals

    Realise that harmony and happiness do not depend solely upon possessions. Build the mental attitude of the result desired, then visualise that result as accomplished and identify the service or action that supports it.

  17. Week 17 of 24

    Intuitive Perception through Concentration

    Relax completely and avoid anxiety about results. Let thought dwell on one worthy object or quality until you are identified with it and temporarily conscious of nothing else.

  18. Week 18 of 24

    The Law of Attraction

    Concentrate on your power to create. Seek a logical basis for faith by contemplating how physical life depends upon air and how, in Haanel’s philosophy, spiritual life depends upon an omnipresent creative intelligence.

  19. Week 19 of 24

    Raising your Life Force

    Become fully absorbed in the thought that appearances can be deceptive: the earth appears flat, the sun appears to move and matter appears fixed. Contemplate the deeper reality of constant motion and change.

  20. Week 20 of 24

    Thinking as the True Business in Life

    In the Silence, contemplate the idea that life exists within an all-pervading source: ‘In Him we live and move and have our being.’ Translate the reflection into one constructive choice today.

  21. Week 21 of 24

    Thinking Big Thoughts as the Secret of Success

    Concentrate on Truth and the idea that correct principles make you freer. In silence, awaken the highest conception of truth you can hold and identify how to express it practically.

  22. Week 22 of 24

    New Thinking, New Man

    Contemplate Tennyson’s idea that the Divine is ‘closer than breathing’. Rest in calm connection, then choose one evidence-based action that supports your health or recovery.

  23. Week 23 of 24

    A Money Consciousness in Service of Mankind

    Concentrate on the fact that you are not merely a body with a spirit, but a spiritual being expressing through a body. Examine one financial goal and define the service and value it must represent.

  24. Week 24 of 24

    The Truth shall set you free

    Contemplate the wonder of the world, your own capacity and the growing human knowledge of truth. Review the 24 weeks, select the four practices that changed you most and commit them to a 90-day plan.

Exercise descriptions are presented as concise study summaries, based on the weekly exercise material in the public-domain 24-part work and the UYP study curriculum. For the complete lesson text, consult your own authorised copy.
