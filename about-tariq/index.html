ABOUT TARIQ

About Tariq

I know what it feels like when life changes before you feel ready.

My journey has taken me from London to Barcelona, through family life and an international career in partner sales—and, more recently, into the uncertainty of starting again after an unexpected career change.

For years, I worked with distributors, partners and customers across the globe, helping people build relationships, develop opportunities and turn shared goals into action. That experience taught me how much difference it makes when people feel supported, see a clear next step and believe their contribution matters.

But when my own circumstances changed, I had to bring that same determination closer to home. I had to rethink where I was directing my attention, what I wanted my next chapter to stand for and how I would keep moving forward.

That experience deepened my interest in personal development. Through studying the Master Key System (MKS) and The Power, I began exploring two connected questions: What am I giving my attention to? And what kind of person do I want to be as I face what comes next?

The MKS taught me to practise stillness, concentration and more deliberate thought. The Power encouraged me to bring greater love, gratitude and appreciation into the way I relate to people and daily life.

Before, my attention could be pulled towards uncertainty, what had gone wrong or how far I still had to go. A goal could feel distant, and a setback could make it harder to see my next step.

Now, I’m learning to pause and choose where I place my attention. I can recognise what I appreciate, picture the direction I want to move in and focus on one useful action. When difficulties arise, I can acknowledge them, learn from them and keep going with greater purpose.

That doesn’t mean every problem disappears, or that thoughts alone decide what happens. It means I’m practising how to meet changing circumstances—with more awareness, appreciation and discipline, and with a clearer sense of the next step I can take.

I created Unleash Your Power to share that practical process with people who feel stuck, unfocused or unsure how to move forward. Through structured study, reflection and consistent practice, I help people strengthen their focus, reconnect with a meaningful goal and take deliberate steps towards the life they want to build.

I’m not here to promise overnight transformation. I’m here to share what I’m learning and walk alongside people as they put it into practice—one thought, one choice and one step at a time.

Your next chapter doesn’t have to begin with everything figured out. It can begin with one clear decision—and the willingness to follow through.
