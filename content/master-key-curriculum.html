WEEK 01One Consciousness - One Power＋

Introduction

Welcome to the beginning of your journey. Over the next 24 weeks, you are going to explore something most people spend very little time thinking about: the way you use your own mind. Your thoughts, emotions, words, habits and actions are influencing your experience every day. The purpose of this programme is to help you become more aware of them and begin directing them with greater purpose.
